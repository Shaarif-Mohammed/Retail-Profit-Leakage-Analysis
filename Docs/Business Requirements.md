## **Business Problem & Requirements**
#### **Retail Profit Leakage & Discount Analysis**

### Project Overview

Retail businesses generate thousands of transactions every day across multiple products, customers, regions, and sales channels. While overall revenue and profit provide a high-level view of business performance, they often fail to reveal hidden operational issues that negatively impact profitability. One such challenge is **profit leakage**—the gradual loss of potential profit caused by unprofitable products, excessive discounting, inefficient pricing strategies, and regional performance variations.

This project aims to investigate these hidden sources of profit leakage by transforming raw transactional data into actionable business insights. Using SQL for data preparation and analysis, followed by Power BI for interactive reporting, the project demonstrates how data analytics can help organizations move beyond revenue reporting and make profitability-driven business decisions.

---

### Business Problem

Although the business generates strong sales, management lacks visibility into the underlying factors affecting profitability. Traditional reports provide overall sales and profit figures but do not explain:

* Which products consistently generate losses.
* Whether discounting strategies improve or reduce profitability.
* Which regions and markets underperform.
* How profit leakage changes over time.
* Which business areas require management attention.

Without these insights, decision-makers risk focusing on revenue growth while overlooking operational inefficiencies that reduce overall business performance.

---

### Business Requirements

The reporting solution should enable business stakeholders to:

* Measure overall business performance using key sales and profitability metrics.
* Identify and quantify profit leakage across products, categories, and regions.
* Evaluate the impact of discounting strategies on profitability.
* Monitor historical performance using time-based trend analysis.
* Investigate business performance at multiple levels of detail through interactive reporting.
* Support data-driven decision making using clear visualizations and analytical insights.

The solution should present information in a format suitable for both executive-level monitoring and operational investigation.

---

### Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

* Build an end-to-end retail analytics solution using SQL and Power BI.
* Design a dimensional data model optimized for business reporting.
* Develop reusable DAX measures for KPI reporting and advanced analytics.
* Create an interactive dashboard that supports exploratory analysis.
* Identify the primary drivers of profit leakage through quantitative analysis.
* Deliver executive-level insights supported by analytical evidence.

---

### Scope of Analysis

The analysis focuses on evaluating retail business performance across multiple dimensions, including:

* Sales and profitability
* Products and categories
* Discount effectiveness
* Customer segments
* Geographic performance
* Historical business trends

The project emphasizes profitability analysis rather than purely descriptive sales reporting, with profit leakage serving as the central business theme throughout the investigation.

---

### Solution Approach

The project follows a structured analytics workflow designed to mirror a real-world business intelligence implementation.

1. Assess and understand the source data.
2. Design and implement a dimensional data model.
3. Load and validate data using SQL.
4. Perform exploratory and business-focused analysis.
5. Develop reusable DAX measures and KPIs.
6. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
7. Analyze findings to identify root causes of profit leakage.
8. Summarize key business insights and strategic recommendations.

Each stage builds upon the previous one, ensuring that business conclusions are supported by validated data and a robust analytical foundation.

---

### Expected Outcomes

Upon completion, the project will provide business stakeholders with the ability to:

* Understand where profit leakage occurs within the organization.
* Evaluate the effectiveness of current discounting practices.
* Identify high-risk products, categories, and regions.
* Monitor profitability trends over time.
* Support strategic decision-making with data-driven insights.
* Improve overall business performance by focusing on the areas with the greatest opportunity for profitability improvement.

---

### Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it delivers:
